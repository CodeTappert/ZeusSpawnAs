# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-14

### Added

- "Spawn As" strip in the Zeus Create panel: newly placed units and groups join BLUFOR, OPFOR, Independent or Civilian the moment they're placed, or keep their own side (DEFAULT).
- Banner under the mission clock while a side is active.
- CBA keybind to cycle the side, default Ctrl+Shift+S.
